About me

I am a PhD researcher in Civil Engineering at The University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA). My work brings together urban climate science, environmental equity, geospatial analysis, and computational methods to understand how heat exposure varies across urban communities.

My research integrates physics-based computational modeling, artificial intelligence, and geospatial analytics to advance the sustainability, climate resilience, and energy efficiency of the built environment. By combining computational fluid dynamics (CFD), deep reinforcement learning (DRL), satellite remote sensing, and spatial analysis, I develop computational and data-driven approaches to improve building environmental performance, investigate urban heat exposure and inequalities, and evaluate climate mitigation strategies across building and neighborhood scales.

My broader interests include urban microclimates, indoor environmental quality, building energy performance, and data-driven approaches to climate-resilient urban design.
