Behind the research / 03

Hi, I'm Quang.

I am a PhD researcher at The University of Texas at San Antonio, working at the intersection of urban environmental science, built environment research, and computational methods.

My research explores unequal urban heat exposure and its relationship to historical neighborhood policies, contemporary socioeconomic vulnerability, and urban form. I also investigate simulation-driven strategies for improving outdoor and indoor thermal conditions.

My broader goal is to build rigorous, transferable evidence that informs equitable climate adaptation and resilient urban design.
