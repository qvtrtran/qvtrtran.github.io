Year-round heat exposure and structural spatial vulnerability: A case study of San Antonio, Texas

Quang Van Tran, Parisa Najafian, Farzad Hashemi, Tobi Eniolu Morakinyo, Esteban López Ochoa

A satellite-based investigation of year-round thermal patterns and social vulnerability in San Antonio, combining land surface temperature with vegetation, population, and socioeconomic indicators.
